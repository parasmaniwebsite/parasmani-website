Bureau of Indian Standards, Surat Branch Office. Our Ref: SUBO/CM/L 7100071995, Dated: 27-09-2024.

Licensee: M/S PARASMANI TUBES PVT LTD, 5/2 GIDC PHASE 1, UMBERGAON, UMARGAM - 396171, GUJARAT
Product: Wrought Copper Tubes For Refrigeration And Air-Conditioning Purposes, IS 10773 : 1995
Grade/Class/Type/Variety: "Wrought Copper Tubes For Refrigeration And Air Conditioning Purposes, Grade-2, Condition - Soft Annealed And Light Drawn, Size- Outside Diameter From 6.35 Mm To Upto And Including 41.30 Mm, Wall Thickness From 0.45 Mm To Upto And Including 1.43 Mm"
"The number assigned to this licence is CM/L- 7100071995 which has been made operative from 2024-09-12 and is valid upto 2025-09-11."
note: Parasmani confirmed on 13 Nov 2026 that this licence number is current and was renewed. The renewal document is not in the repo, so this copy's validity dates are out of date: do not approve the "valid upto 2025-09-11" line. (1) Validity on this copy ended 2025-09-11. (2) It covers IS 10773 : 1995, but the TDS and Quality Policy say IS 10773 : 2025. (3) Its scope is soft annealed and light drawn, 6.35–41.30 mm OD, 0.45–1.43 mm wall only. Hard drawn tubes, larger sizes and other walls are outside it, yet the TDS marking example shows "IS 10773 : 2025 · Cu-DHP · HARD DRAWN". See REVIEW.md A-18.

## KB-10-13 Dimensional tolerances (HVAC/R TDS)
status: needs_verification
safety_critical: true
source: frontend/src/assets/downloads/Spec-Sheets/Parasmani-HVAC-R-Copper-Tubes-TDS.pdf (section 5)
---
"Dimensional tolerances are as specified in IS 10773 : 2025. Outside diameter, wall thickness and length are otherwise as mutually agreed between purchaser and supplier."
5.1 Tolerance on mean wall thickness (mm), by OD band:
| Wall Thickness, WT (mm) | 3.0 < OD ≤ 16 | 16 < OD ≤ 25 | 25 < OD ≤ 50 | 50 < OD ≤ 100 | 100 < OD ≤ 180 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 < WT ≤ 0.40 | ± 0.03 | ± 0.04 | ± 0.05 | — | — |
| 0.40 < WT ≤ 0.60 | ± 0.05 | ± 0.05 | ± 0.06 | — | — |
| 0.60 < WT ≤ 0.90 | ± 0.06 | ± 0.06 | ± 0.08 | ± 0.10 | — |
| 0.90 < WT ≤ 1.5 | ± 0.08 | ± 0.09 | ± 0.09 | ± 0.10 | ± 0.20 |
| 1.5 < WT ≤ 2.0 | ± 0.09 | ± 0.10 | ± 0.10 | ± 0.15 | ± 0.20 |
| 2.0 < WT ≤ 3.0 | ± 0.10 | ± 0.10 | ± 0.10 | ± 0.20 | ± 0.20 |
5.2 Tolerance on mean outside diameter: 3.0 < OD ≤ 16: ± 0.05; 16 < OD ≤ 25: ± 0.06; 25 < OD ≤ 50: ± 0.08; 50 < OD ≤ 75: ± 0.10; 75 < OD ≤ 100: ± 0.12; 100 < OD ≤ 125: ± 0.15; 125 < OD ≤ 150: ± 0.18
5.3 Length tolerance (straight lengths): l ≤ 600: +1.6 / −0; 600 < l ≤ 2 000: +2.0 / −0; 2 000 < l ≤ 4 000: +5.0 / −0; 4 000 < l ≤ 7 000: +8.0 / −0; l > 7 000: +10.0 / −0. "Minus tolerances on length are not permissible."
5.4 Roundness: t/D up to 0.03: 1.5%; over 0.03 to 0.05: 1.1%; over 0.05 to 0.10: 0.8% or 0.5 mm, whichever is more; over 0.10: 0.7% or 0.05 mm, whichever is more. "These tolerances apply only to light and hard drawn tubes."
