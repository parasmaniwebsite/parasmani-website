## KB-01-11 Manufacturing process
status: needs_verification
safety_critical: false
source: frontend/src/assets/downloads/Spec-Sheets/Parasmani-HVAC-R-Copper-Tubes-TDS.pdf (section 7)
---
Tubes are drawn in-house from tube shell, billet or bar. No tube is manufactured from used tube material. The process sequence is:
Copper melting and casting → extrusion → drawing → cutting → straightening / coiling → annealing → visual inspection → eddy current and other tests → packing and dispatch
